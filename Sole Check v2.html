      • Proof of purchase (receipt or order email)

      • A close-up of the failure with something for scale

      How v1 decides
      
  
      Fixed rules, no AI model in the loop yet. Each failure type has an "early" distance: outsole separation 400 km, foam split 300 km, worn lugs 250 km, upper tear 300 km, seam or eyelet 400 km, dead cushion 250 km. Failing well before that points toward a defect; well after points toward wear. Snags, road use and abrasive terrain push toward wear. A replacement pair failing the same way pushes toward defect. These thresholds are our starting assumptions from one r/trailrunning thread and retailer reviews. They have not been validated.
